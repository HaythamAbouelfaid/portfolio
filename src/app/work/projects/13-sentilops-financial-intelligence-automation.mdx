## Overview

**Sentilops** is my financial-literacy and intelligence automation platform.

The system is designed to convert an overwhelming stream of newsletters, market updates, business content, investing education, and real-estate information into structured research that can be searched, routed, and acted on.

The goal is **financial intelligence and education**, not automated investment advice or guaranteed-return trading signals.

## Architecture

A representative Sentilops automation uses a pipeline such as:

1. Incoming webhook / source content
2. Variable normalization
3. Historical context lookup
4. AI agent with a finance knowledge layer
5. Web content retrieval and text normalization
6. Second-stage AI analysis
7. Structured routing
8. Sheets / Drive / Slack outputs

## Decision Routes

Depending on the analysis, the system can route information into:

- priority alerts
- action queues
- tool / platform trackers
- market lessons
- cash-flow diagram inputs
- research archives
- structured finance records

## Knowledge Layer

Instead of treating each article as an isolated summary, Sentilops is designed to preserve useful material as a growing financial knowledge system.

This creates reusable context for:

- financial literacy
- assets vs. liabilities
- cash-flow thinking
- real-estate research
- business trends
- opportunity tracking
- finance content creation

## Reliability Patterns

The architecture uses structured routing and dedicated outputs rather than one giant AI response.

That makes it easier to:

- inspect each decision path
- keep research organized
- send only meaningful alerts
- separate action items from educational notes
- preserve source material
- troubleshoot individual branches
