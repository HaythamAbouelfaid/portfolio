## Overview

I built a family of finance and real-estate intelligence workflows in Make.com to reduce the manual work required to read, classify, store, and act on recurring information sources.

The automation family includes systems built around financial-literacy and real-estate content streams such as:

- BiggerPockets-oriented research
- Rich Dad-style cash-flow education
- real-estate newsletters
- market and business updates
- internal finance notes
- opportunity research

## Common Architecture

Although each source has its own logic, the reusable pattern is:

1. **Ingest** — receive newsletter, email, row, webhook, or source item.
2. **Normalize** — extract usable text and structured fields.
3. **Analyze** — apply AI classification against a finance / real-estate knowledge framework.
4. **Score** — evaluate relevance, priority, opportunity fit, and actionability.
5. **Route** — send the item to the right branch.
6. **Store** — persist structured records and research files.
7. **Alert** — surface only higher-value items when appropriate.
8. **Reuse** — feed useful knowledge into future research and content.

## Why I Built It

Reading every source manually does not scale.

The more important challenge is not summarization — it is deciding:

- what matters
- why it matters
- where it belongs
- what should happen next
- what should be preserved for future context

That is the part the automation architecture is designed around.

## Real-Estate Angle

The system supports a real-estate and cash-flow lens without making investment decisions automatically.

Useful outputs can include:

- educational property / cash-flow notes
- market lessons
- deal-research queues
- opportunity watchlists
- business-model observations
- structured source archives
